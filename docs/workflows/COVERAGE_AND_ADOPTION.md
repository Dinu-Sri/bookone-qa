## Continuous maintenance

Every new feature/edge gets workflow-change/intake record and traceability before closure. Update additive variants/newfamily IDs;retain old IDs and immutable runs. Revisit required layers when a shared dependency changes. Maintain coverage by profile×rule×transition×risk×layer;report gaps and outstanding expert/owner decisions rather than an overall readiness percentage.

A weekly review is recommended during development and required before releases;no background scheduled task is created. Retire only with support decision,compatibility impact,replacement and retained history. Do not let outdated top-level status counts override newer exact candidate evidence.
