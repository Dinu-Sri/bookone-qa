## Mandatory maintenance clarification

The owner explicitly requires maintenance, editing, updating, expansion, correction, fixing and audit as a MUST. Read [AGENTS rules73–81](../AGENTS.md#mandatory-workbook-maintenance-correction-and-audit) and CONTRIBUTING. Every relevant feature/bug/edge/documentation discrepancy triggers impact review, independently justified expected results, canonical-source edits, stable IDs/revisions, independent review, semantic audit, structural validation and durable change/handoff records. Preserve original failures and distinguish fixing an incorrect specification from fixing the ERP. The shared rulebook plus business-specific expected outputs form the maintained reference against which actual candidate ERP behavior is tested. Mandatory procedure does not certify draft examples or unexecuted cases.
